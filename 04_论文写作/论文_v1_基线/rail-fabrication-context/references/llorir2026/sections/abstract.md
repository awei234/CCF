# Abstract

Perspective paper arguing that denoising — maximizing usable evidence density and verifiability within a context window — is becoming the primary bottleneck across the information access pipeline, since LLMs are uniquely vulnerable to noise that directly causes hallucinations and reasoning failures. Conceptualizes a four-stage framework (inaccessible to undiscoverable, to misaligned, to unverifiable) and a pipeline-organized taxonomy of signal-to-noise optimization techniques spanning indexing, retrieval, context engineering, verification and agentic workflow.
