# Abstract

Audits 111 million references across 2.5 million papers in arXiv, bioRxiv, SSRN and PubMed Central, finding a sharp rise in non-existent references following widespread LLM adoption, with a conservative estimate of 146,932 hallucinated citations in 2025 alone. Errors are diffuse, pronounced in fields with rapid AI uptake and in manuscripts with linguistic signatures of AI-assisted writing; hallucinated references disproportionately credit prominent and male scholars. Preprint moderation and journal processes capture only a fraction of errors.
