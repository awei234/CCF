# Abstract

Two-year community roadmap for trustworthy autonomous science. Notes a sobering counter-current to progress: corrected flagship discovery results, benchmarks showing agents complete only a fraction of open-ended research, and fabricated citations surfacing at leading venues. Defines the field's defining tension as a verification asymmetry — producing a candidate discovery is no longer the hard part, verifying it is. Elevates trust, verification and reproducibility, plus safety/security/governance, to first-class status.
