# Do Validation Rails Curb LLM Research-Agent Fabrication? A Controlled Context-Engineering Experiment

> 团队：Prototype ｜ 版本：v5 ｜ SAR：5.8 / 10（ICLR）

## Abstract

LLM research agents are prone to fabricating citations, numbers, and execution claims. We treat process-level validation rails (F02 plan validation, F03 results cross-checking, F04 citation verification) as a context-engineering intervention and measure their causal effect on fabrication rates under a fixed pipeline. In a fully controlled design with fixed tasks, model, and seeds, we show that closed-pool tasks are at floor, while an adversarial writing task (T3) that disables online verification produces real fabrication in the no-rail arm (CFR up to 0.0769, NFR up to 0.1071). Full-rail, prompt-only, and F03-only reduce both to 0.0; F04-only removes citation fabrication but leaves residual numeric fabrication. We report single-gate ablations, costs, and limitations.
